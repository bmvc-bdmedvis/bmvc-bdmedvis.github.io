## Review policy

Plan for two to three reviews per submission plus an area-chair or senior
organiser meta-review. Do not add a formal rebuttal period unless the primary
organiser revises the schedule. Resolve borderline papers through reviewer and
area-chair discussion, with targeted author clarification only when necessary.
